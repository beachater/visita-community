# Visita — Mini Brand Manual

*Step Inside From Anywhere*

Colors · Typography · Purpose · Meaning

## 01 — Name & tagline

**Brand name:** Visita

**Tagline:** *Step Inside From Anywhere*

### Purpose

Visita is a web application that lets users virtually explore cafe interiors before visiting in person. Using 3D spatial mapping and Gaussian Splat technology, Visita captures real cafe spaces and transforms them into interactive walkthroughs — letting users see actual seating, layout, amenities, and atmosphere from any device, anywhere.

### Meaning

Visita comes from the Filipino word for visit — bisita — and shares roots with Spanish. It means to go, to see, to arrive. The name signals intention: not passive browsing, but active, purposeful exploration. It is warm, human, and immediately understood across Filipino and broader Southeast Asian audiences.

The tagline — Step Inside From Anywhere — completes the promise. Visita is the invitation. The tagline is the experience. Together they communicate: you don't have to be there to know it's the right place.

## 02 — Color palette

The color system is built on the fusion of two brand directions: warm Filipino humanity (Direction 08) and professional platform credibility (Direction 12). The result is a palette that feels welcoming to users and trustworthy to cafe owners.

| Color | Hex |
|---|---|
| Deep Navy | `#042C53` |
| Ocean | `#185FA5` |
| Sky | `#378ADD` |
| Mist | `#B5D4F4` |
| Amber | `#EF9F27` |
| Gold | `#FAC775` |
| Cloud (base) | `#E6F1FB` |

### Primary — blue family

Deep Navy, Ocean, and Sky form the primary palette. Navy anchors all major surfaces and communicates authority and trust — this is the color cafe owners experience. Ocean and Sky support headings, links, and interactive elements. Together the blue family signals: this is a serious, reliable platform.

### Accent — amber family

Amber and Gold are used sparingly — for calls to action, highlights, and key interactive moments. Amber is the light inside the cafe, the warmth of arrival, the color of hope replacing disappointment. It should never dominate; its power comes from restraint. Use it only where the eye must land.

### Base — Cloud

Cloud (`#E6F1FB`) is the canvas. It provides breathing room across all backgrounds and UI surfaces, keeping the experience feeling open, transparent, and clean.

## 03 — Typography

Visita uses two typefaces that work as a pair — one warm and editorial, one clean and functional. Both are available free on Google Fonts.

| Role | Typeface | Weights |
|---|---|---|
| Display / headlines | **Playfair Display** | Bold · Semibold · Italic |
| Body / UI / tagline | **DM Sans** | Regular · Medium · Bold |

### Playfair Display — why

Playfair Display brings editorial warmth and craft to Visita's headlines. Its high contrast strokes and elegant serifs signal premium quality and thoughtful design — the same feeling a beautifully designed cafe gives you when you walk in. It is the typeface that earns trust through beauty.

### DM Sans — why

DM Sans handles all body copy, UI text, buttons, captions, and the tagline. It is clean, geometric, and highly legible at small sizes — essential for a web app that needs to communicate clearly across mobile and desktop. Its slight humanist warmth prevents it from feeling cold or clinical.

### Type scale

| Level | Typeface | Size | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero | Playfair Display Bold | 40–48px | Hero headlines, cover slides |
| Heading 1 | Playfair Display Bold | 28–32px | Section titles |
| Heading 2 | Playfair Display Semibold | 20–24px | Card headers, sub-sections |
| Body | DM Sans Regular | 14–16px | Paragraphs, descriptions |
| UI / Button | DM Sans Medium | 13–14px | Buttons, labels, nav |
| Caption | DM Sans Regular | 11–12px | Tagline, metadata, hints |

## 04 — Brand voice

Visita speaks like a trusted local friend who has already scouted the place for you. It is warm without being informal, honest without being cold, and confident without being arrogant.

| Visita sounds like | Visita does not sound like |
|---|---|
| Warm and inviting | Cold or corporate |
| Honest and transparent | Overly technical |
| Confident and purposeful | Generic or global |
| Local and grounded | A replacement for visiting |

## 05 — Usage rules

### Color

Navy is the dominant color — it should lead on hero sections, headers, and primary surfaces. Amber is the accent — use it only for the most important interactive element on any given screen (one CTA, one highlight). Never use amber as a background for large areas. Cloud is always the page background.

### Typography

Playfair Display is for display and headline use only — never for body copy or captions. DM Sans handles everything functional. Do not mix more than two weights of DM Sans in a single layout. The tagline — Step Inside From Anywhere — is always set in DM Sans, never Playfair.

### Name

The brand name is always written as Visita — title case, never all-caps, never lowercase. The tagline is always written as Step Inside From Anywhere — title case. On dark backgrounds, the brand name appears in white or Cloud. On light backgrounds, it appears in Deep Navy.
