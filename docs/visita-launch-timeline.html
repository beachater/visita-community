Visita launch timeline

From today to a public launch in Cagayan de Oro on Tuesday 29 December: the core visitor and venue-owner flows first, then stakeholder testing, fixes, an open beta, and a final round of fixes.

Still to come from the client side: the final UI/UX and branding, and the business model. Until they arrive, screens use the PRD's provisional colours and type, and pricing uses placeholder founder rates. The dates each one is needed by are in the chart and under "Waiting on the client or project head".

What gets built, in order

Tier 1

Before stakeholder testing · 28 Sep – 6 Nov · provisional look until the final branding arrives

Visitors

  • Map with amber (scanned) and white pins, search across Visita's own cafes, filters, Top Picks, open now, distance map approach pending
  • Saved cafes, recently viewed, "I want to see inside"
  • The walkthrough with its info panel, amenity cards, scan date, menu, share and deep links
  • Directions hand off to Google Maps (Apple Maps option on iPhone)
  • Google sign-in and reviews with rating and photo

Venue owners

  • Registration with manual approval
  • Listing editor, occupancy, events, review replies
  • Analytics: views, walkthrough opens, directions taps, search appearances, peak hours, filters, demand

Admin and legal

  • Venue management, scan uploads, owner approvals, amenity edit queue, review moderation
  • Privacy policy, terms and storage consent (RA 10173)

Tier 2

After Tier 1, aimed at launch · 9 Nov – 4 Dec

  • Amenity verification: visitors confirm or report outdated details (no points yet)
  • Menu photo capture using OCR and a free-tier AI; typing the menu stays available
  • Billing, handled manually: admin records payments, owners see their status waits on business model
  • Marketing pages: home sections, For Cafes pricing, About, Contact, FAQ pricing waits on business model
  • Offline support with a stale-data notice
  • Admin demand queue
  • Google Places search fallback waits on approval

Tier 3

After launch · waits on cafe-owner interviews

  • Points, awards, badges, check-ins
  • Referral codes, vouchers and boosted listings waits on business model
  • Community page: leaderboard, most-awarded reviews
  • Owner monthly report, visitor monthly recap
  • Fraud monitoring, subscription and churn view, audit log

If things slip

  • Checkpoints show slippage early. The visitor flow is due 23 Oct and the owner and admin screens 6 Nov, both on check-in days.
  • Tier 2 gives way first. Anything in Tier 2 not ready by 4 Dec moves to after launch, without moving the beta or launch dates.
  • Late inputs move their own work, not the launch. If the branding arrives after 20 Nov, the beta runs on the provisional look and the design pass moves into fix round 2 or after launch. If the business model is late, billing stays manual and the pricing page shows "contact us".
  • Tier 1 is tight for one developer. If checkpoint A slips by more than a week, we'll agree at that check-in which Tier 1 items to simplify.
